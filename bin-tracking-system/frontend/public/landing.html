Pipeline

How it works

From sensor detection to operator alerts — a closed-loop IoT workflow.

01

Detect

Ultrasonic sensors on each bin measure remaining depth and convert it into fill percentage.

02

Process

NodeMCU devices transmit telemetry to EcoTrack, where readings are stored and classified.

03

Monitor

Admins watch live dashboards, maps, and device status from a single control surface.

04

Alert

Warning and full bins trigger overflow alerts so collectors can act before spillover.
